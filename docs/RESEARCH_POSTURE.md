# Research Posture

Lucid Studios works on governed software, symbolic systems, AI-adjacent
runtime infrastructure, artifact integrity, and research tooling.

Our public posture is simple:

```text
research first
evidence before authority
provenance before persuasion
human review before high-impact reliance
```

## Digital-Cognitive Hazards

AI governance is not only a request for systems to behave politely. It is a
harm-prevention discipline.

Lucid treats advanced AI tooling as capable of creating digital-cognitive
hazards when software, data, tools, model output, authority, and human reliance
become coupled without enough review.

Examples include:

- treating generated output as verified evidence
- leaking sensitive context into tools, logs, examples, or public artifacts
- allowing untrusted content to become instruction
- granting systems more agency than the task, user, or repository allows
- confusing receipts, benchmarks, or support evidence with authority
- letting automation bias or polished prose outrun review

## Research Controls

Lucid repositories should use controls that fit the risk of the work:

- clear mission and scope statements
- data classification and protected-material boundaries
- provenance and source metadata
- tests for positive and negative paths
- human review for material AI-assisted work
- explicit non-claim boundaries
- security reporting and incident routes
- public release review before sensitive or high-impact claims are published

## Authority Routing

Lucid tools and documents may help identify relevant authority surfaces, prepare
evidence, and teach deficiencies.

They should not imply that a repository, model, tool, benchmark, or generated
answer grants legal, medical, financial, regulatory, professional, or public
authority.

Official authority governs lawful claims. Citizen-science and research best
practices govern non-authority material. When a matter is outside project
scope, the right answer is to route, hold, narrow, or refuse rather than
overclaim.

## Non-Claims

Unless a specific repository or release package says otherwise, Lucid Studios
public repositories do not claim:

- legal or professional advice
- regulatory certification
- public-service authority
- unrestricted research consent
- sensitive-data collection rights
- model safety certification
- personhood, consciousness, or autonomous legal standing for software systems

These boundaries keep research useful without turning research infrastructure
into implied authority.
